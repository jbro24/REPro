Career Commander — Phase 9 Deterministic Evidence Matcher
Implementation transcript
Date: 2026-10-05

Implementation
- Added deterministic matching in src/matcher.js and persisted MatchRuns plus MatchResults.
- Matching uses explicit normalized skill relationships and evidence links only.
- VERIFIED requires both a VERIFIED skill state and linked VERIFIED evidence.
- LEARNING produces PARTIAL when relevant; missing or unsupported evidence produces MISSING.
- Every result contains requirement identity, priority, evidence summaries, state, and an explanation.

Boundary
- The matcher consumes validated JobRequirements and candidate-owned Skills/Evidence.
- It performs no LLM call, calculates no overall score, and does not infer production experience from names, education, URLs, or aspirations.

Tests
- Covered MATCH, PARTIAL, MISSING, unrelated evidence, linked evidence, required/preferred priority, repeatability, ownership, and the absence of score output.

Status
- PASS. Phase 9 behavior is exercised by tests/pipeline.test.js.
